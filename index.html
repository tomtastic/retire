How this model works

Monthly modelling. Investments grow and withdrawals occur monthly. Results are grouped into twelve-month projection years.

Tax-efficient ordering. Stocks, ISA and cash bridge pension age. Thereafter, pension withdrawals use available Personal Allowance before the non-pension pot is spent.

State Pension. State Pension replaces part of the portfolio withdrawal rather than increasing the target income.

Tax limits. ISA withdrawals are tax-free. CGT, dividend tax, NI, charges beyond the entered real return, and future tax-rule changes are not modelled.

This is an illustrative planning tool, not regulated financial advice. Real investment returns are volatile and tax rules can change.
